Originally posted in August 2023, in the days after the Lahaina wildfire.

In the wake of the devastating wildfires that have swept through, and literally wiped out an entire town of homes and businesses (9.2 square miles with a 13,000+ population of primarily area workers who support the nearly 25,000 additional near daily tourist population . . . unimaginable challenges are now faced by those left homeless (estimated to be about 15,000+). There are an additional 5,000 to 7,000+ people who live just beyond the fire ravaged area, but who are also displaced by no utilities and being area landlocked behind the mandatory evacuation area.

Livable housing on Maui is a challenge in good times, and now with the instant homelessness of the approximate 15,000+ residents, a true crisis exists. Those that have escaped their fire ravaged homes lost everything and left with the clothing on their backs. Over 60 people have been confirmed as deceased, and nearly 1,000 remain unaccounted for.

Not only is there an uncertainty of where these ordinary, everyday people will lay their heads to sleep, shower, and eat . . . but most have medical supply needs such as pharmacy, equipment, and dietary.

Who we are

We are Code Critical Logistics, here to help the displaced . . . we are a Global Carrier Network of associated companies which offers Time-Critical Transportation Services to meet client needs without compromise to Safety, Quality, Freshness, or On-Time Delivery Commitments.

Our primary focus has always been on movement of Urgent need Medical Supplies, Equipment, and Donor Organ Transports; although we do move whatever any individual or business has need to be moved quickly. We employ Air Transport, Sea Transport, Land Transport and are fortunate enough to own most of our own transport equipment (or have contracts for instant use of).

Our immediate mission is to bridge the gap between ‘needs’ and ‘availability’ and provide for any and all of the Logistics necessary (the coordination, pickup, transport, and delivery) to any critical need items that the displaced have urgent need for (ie; medical supplies, pharmaceuticals, including biohazard, and hazmat designated material. And while the nature of our business is highly individualized, it can be an expensive and daunting task for anyone to source, fulfill, transport and distribute. Some transports are advance stock in nature, but the majority are individually focused needs.
