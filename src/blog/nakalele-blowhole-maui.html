Nakalele Point and Blowhole, and the area just beyond; the tiny and wholly residential village of Kahakuloa (no businesses, and no services) . . . definitely an infrequent goto for anyone, Islander or Visitor because the West End Road is mostly single lane (blind curves, mostly without shoulders, pull asides, or guard rails and with steep drop offs, rock spitting mountain walls) and which is a simply downright scary and treacherous 25+ mile drive.

Beautiful Scapes, Hiking and Views the entire way, and definitely worth the vehicle adventure if you can stomach the few faint moments of meet and greets as you encounter an occasional meetup with head on traffic, approaching from around the many blind curves.

Before you go

  • Kahakuloa has no businesses or services, so fill up on gas, water and snacks before you set out.
  • Much of the road is a single lane with blind curves. Drive slowly and be ready to pull over for oncoming traffic.
  • Check your rental car agreement before driving it. Ask us if you'd like help booking a car for the trip.
