Can I book just one part of my trip?

Yes. We can book only your hotel, only flights, only a rental car or activities, or put the whole trip together.

Can I book flights myself?

Yes. Use our flight booking portal to buy tickets yourself, or find the flights you want and call us, and one of our staff will book them for you.

Do you plan business travel?

Yes. We plan and book both business trips and personal vacations, including hotels, flights and ground transportation.

Are you a licensed travel agency?

Yes. Vacations4Less is accredited as a stand-alone agency by the International Airlines Travel Agent Network (IATAN) and is a member of the Cruise Lines International Association (CLIA).
